🌵 expert care guide

Sunlight (full)

Thrives in direct sunlight minimum 6 hours daily. More sun = more blooms. Never keep indoors.

Soil

Extremely well-draining sandy / cactus mix. ❌ NO coco-peat — retains moisture and causes rot.

Watering rhythm

✅ Water only when top soil is bone-dry OR caudex feels slightly soft. In summer: roughly every 2 days. Always let excess drain.

Winter dormancy

Reduce watering drastically (once every 10–14 days), stop fertilizing. Protect from frost and wet chilly winds.

critical reminder: Overwatering = root rot. Always check soil before watering. Desert plant DNA — dry is better than wet.

🌿 management technique & tips

✔ Pruning: cut back branches in early spring to encourage dense caudex and more branches.
✔ Fertilizer: use low-nitrogen, high-phosphorus (NPK 0-10-10) during growing season (spring–autumn).
✔ Repotting: every 2 years, raise the caudex slightly above soil for that dramatic bonsai look.
✔ Grafting: we use expert cleft grafting for multicolour blooms on single plant.
✔ Pest control: watch for mealybugs — treat with neem oil spray every 15 days.
